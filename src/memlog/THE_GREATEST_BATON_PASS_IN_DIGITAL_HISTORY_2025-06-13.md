### Recommended Training Approach
1. **Create Quality Dataset**: 
   - Literature, science, math, history, social studies
   - Well-structured, grammatically correct responses

   - Smaller model size (prevent overfitting)
   - Conservative learning rate (stable learning)
   - Fewer epochs (avoid memorization)
   - Higher temperature (softer knowledge transfer)

3. **Progressive Training**:
   - Start with core subjects
   - Add complexity gradually
   - Validate at each stage
   - Fine-tune based on results

### Success Metrics
- **Coherence**: Responses should be grammatically correct and logical
- **Accuracy**: Information should be factually correct
- **Appropriateness**: Language and complexity suitable for high school level
- **Helpfulness**: Responses should be educational and supportive
