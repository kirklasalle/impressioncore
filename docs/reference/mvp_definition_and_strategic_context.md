## 🎯 MVP Definition

**MVP** stands for **Minimum Viable Product** - a foundational concept in lean startup methodology and product development.

### Formal Definition

An MVP is the version of a product with just enough features to:

1. **Satisfy early customers** and validate the core value proposition
2. **Provide feedback** for future product development
3. **Demonstrate market viability** with minimal resource investment
4. **Test key assumptions** about the product and market fit

### Academic and Industry Context

The MVP concept was popularized by Eric Ries in "The Lean Startup" (2011) and has become a cornerstone of modern product development, particularly in:

- **Technology startups** (Silicon Valley methodology)
- **Agile development** practices
- **Product management** frameworks
- **Investment evaluation** (venture capital assessment)
