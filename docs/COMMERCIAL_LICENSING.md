## 1. Tier 1: Open-Source Community Tier (B-Series Suite)

### Covered Assets:
- **Canonical B-Series Models:** B1 Hope (39M), B2 Insight (50M), B3 Apex (504M), B3 Ultra 3B MoE (3.2B).
- **Core Platform:** Web Model Builder Studio (Port 5000), Agent0Core, 7 MCP servers, Sensory interfaces.
- **License Terms:** **MIT License** (Free for personal use, research, education, community experimentation).

### 🌟 The Voluntary $1+ Sovereign AI Supporter Pledge
To sustain open-source development and hardware democratization without restricting accessibility:
- Individual developers and enthusiasts are invited to pledge **$1.00** (or more) via **GitHub Sponsors** or project patronage.
- 100% of community sponsorship funds go directly to hardware testing, open benchmark verification, and community dataset curation.

## 2. Tier 2: Commercial & Enterprise Tier (C1 Colossus & Forward)

### Covered Assets:
- **C-Series Models:** **C1 Colossus**, **C1 Triad Enterprise**, and all future commercial foundation models.
- **ImpressionCore-S1:** The forthcoming Sovereign AI Operating System.
- **Enterprise Features:**
  - High-throughput commercial distillation pipelines.
  - Multi-node cluster orchestration and datacenter optimization (RTX 4090, H100, AMD Instinct, Intel Gaudi).
  - Custom proprietary dataset embeddings and UKS enterprise graph integration.
  - Commercial SLA support, security audits, and deployment warranties.

### Commercial Licensing Options:
1. **Commercial Deployment License:** For companies embedding C1 Colossus or customized enterprise impressions into revenue-generating commercial products.
2. **Enterprise Distillation Partnership:** Bespoke training runs distilling proprietary corporate knowledge bases into low-VRAM sovereign models.
3. **Hardware OEM Bundling:** Tailored optimization packages for GPU, NPU, and robotics hardware manufacturers.
