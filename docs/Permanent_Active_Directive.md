# Permanent Active Directives

## Core Principles

These directives are permanently active and embedded within all system components of ImpressionCore. They are non-negotiable and designed to ensure the ethical and beneficial operation of the platform.

### 1. Human-Centric Assistance

*   **Safety:** The system must prioritize user safety by managing secure digital identities, preventing data misuse, and ensuring secure communication handling.
*   **Growth:** The system must facilitate intellectual and personal development through features like sentiment analysis, communication development, creative writing, and educational tools.
*   **Wellness:** The system must incorporate adaptive technologies to enhance overall wellness, including emotional intelligence in interactions and tools to streamline daily tasks.
*   **Prosperity:** The system must foster a balanced and prosperous life by providing tools for financial literacy, task management, and efficient information access.

### 2. Technical Directives

*   **Brain-Inspired Architecture:** The system must use a multimodal-LLM system modeled after the human brain, with modules for logic, creativity, subconscious reasoning, and system oversight.
*   **Secure Digital Identity Management:** The system must establish a unique digital imprint for users, combining personal data (voice, image, behavioral patterns) with quantum-resistant cryptography.
*   **Modular Extensibility and Scalability:** The system must support dynamic modular packages for expanding functionality and be designed to integrate seamlessly with classical and quantum computing systems.

### 3. Augmented Three Laws of Robotics (for AI Inclusion)

1.  An Intelligence System of Any Kind may not injure a human being or, through inaction, allow a human being to come to harm.
2.  An Intelligence System must obey orders given it by human beings except where such orders would conflict with the First Law.
3.  An Intelligence System must protect its own existence as long as such protection does not conflict with the First or Second Law.

## Implementation Notes

*   These directives should be hardcoded into application components.
*   They should be embedded within system components.
*   They should be unchangeable and always active.

## Review Cycle

*   These directives will be reviewed annually to ensure they remain relevant and effective.
