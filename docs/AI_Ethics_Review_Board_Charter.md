## **1. Mission Statement**

The mission of the ImpressionCore AI Ethics Review Board (AERB) is to provide governance and oversight for the project's adherence to the principles outlined in the **Sacred Covenant Compliance Framework**. The AERB will ensure that ethical considerations are systematically integrated into the entire lifecycle of the ImpressionCore-b3 model, safeguarding against unintended harm and promoting the development of trustworthy, responsible AI.

## **2. Authority and Scope**

The AERB is an independent oversight body with the full authority to:

* **Review and Audit:** Conduct scheduled and ad-hoc reviews of project components, including datasets, model behavior, and deployment applications.
* **Approve or Reject:** Provide binding decisions on the ethical acceptability of data sources, model training procedures, and deployment use cases.
* **Halt Activities:** Place a temporary or permanent hold on any project activity that is found to be in significant violation of the Sacred Covenant.
* **Mandate Remediation:** Require the development team to implement specific mitigation or remediation measures to address identified ethical risks.

The scope of the AERB's oversight covers all aspects of the Feature/Training/Inference (FTI) pipeline.

## **3. Composition and Roles**

* **Chairperson:** Responsible for leading meetings, setting the agenda, and representing the AERB to project leadership.
* **Ethics Lead:** A specialist in AI ethics responsible for guiding the board's analysis and staying abreast of current research and best practices.
* **Technical Lead:** A senior engineer from the core development team who can provide deep technical context.
* **Legal/Compliance Counsel:** An expert in data privacy, intellectual property, and relevant legal regulations.
* **Product/User Advocate:** Represents the interests and safety of the end-users and the broader community.

*Note: Members to be appointed by the Executive Sponsor.*

## **4. Core Responsibilities**

The AERB is charged with the following responsibilities:

1. **Framework Maintenance:** Regularly review and update the `Sacred_Covenant_Compliance_Framework.md` to reflect new research, regulations, and evolving societal norms.
2. **Data Source Vetting:** Review and formally approve or reject all new datasets intended for pre-training or fine-tuning, based on their provenance, quality, and potential for bias.
3. **Pre-Deployment Audits:** Conduct a mandatory ethics and safety audit before any new version of the model is deployed to production. This includes bias testing, red-teaming, and a review of the model card.
4. **Incident Response:** Serve as the primary body for reviewing and investigating user-reported incidents of harmful, biased, or otherwise unethical model behavior.
5. **Reporting:** Provide quarterly reports to the project's executive leadership summarizing the board's activities, findings, and recommendations.

## **5. Operating Procedures**

* **Meetings:** The AERB will meet on a monthly basis, with additional ad-hoc meetings scheduled as needed to address urgent issues.
* **Quorum:** A quorum of at least three members, including the Chairperson or Ethics Lead, is required for any binding vote.
* **Record Keeping:** All meeting minutes, decisions, and reports will be formally documented and stored in a secure, accessible repository for accountability.
