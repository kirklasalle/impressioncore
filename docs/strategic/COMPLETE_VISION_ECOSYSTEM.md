## 🚀 **The Paradigm Shift Vision**

### Core Strategic Insight

Kirk LaSalle envisions a fundamental market transformation:

1. **Current State**: Users dependent on service-provided LLM technology
2. **Future State**: Users owning personal Small Language Models (SLM) as "Personal Advocates", "Personal Educators", and "personal shields" (not just security but, a lot of the world at large)
3. **Market Impact**: Preserves existing LLM market while creating entirely new personal AI market. The ImpressionCore model is a SLM Small Language model and is deliberately design to have a typical U.S. High School Graduate knowledge with some expert Trade craft and Some Degree level knowledge bild into the core model (compressed and distilled). These models will be able to completely utilize agents and MCP standards.

### Revolutionary Concept: Personal AI Shield

- **SLM Technology**: Compressed and distilled language models. The basic knowledge of a highschool graduate, with some advanced trade and academic knowledge. The ability to train higher impressioncore-iu1 is build to dock and distill knowledge from impressioncore-avt1
- **Personal Ownership**: Users own their AI rather than rent access. ImpressionCore is registered to a single user and then the impression is created. The impressioned LM learns and develops by the user. There are only one registered "impression" per user, forever. This also increases LM value as an asset.
- **Privacy Protection**: Personal shield against data harvesting and emerging security threats, from technologie and otherwise. The single regisered impression is part of the blockchain and quantum security, and creates a greater value to the register and "impressioned" impressioncore model.
- **Human Empowerment**: Forward-looking AI technology that enhances rather than replaces human capability. Focus is to help a human thrive and reach the highest of human endeavors. Help accompany the sick and lost and lonely. Enrich the daily tasking and activities most people will undertake. ImpressionCore can also be applied to animals for study, or any person, place or thing!
