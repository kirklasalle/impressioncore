## 🔄 Risk Management & Contingency Plans

### Technical Risks

**Risk:** Hardware evolution makes GTX 1050 Ti target obsolete

- **Mitigation:** Continuous hardware roadmap monitoring, adaptive optimization
- **Contingency:** Expand to newer hardware generations, maintain backward compatibility

**Risk:** Large language models require increasing computational resources

- **Mitigation:** Advanced compression research, model distillation techniques
- **Contingency:** Hybrid cloud-edge architecture, premium hardware tiers

### Market Risks

**Risk:** Big Tech (Google, Microsoft, Meta) enters personal AI space aggressively

- **Mitigation:** Privacy differentiation, community moat, rapid innovation
- **Contingency:** Partnership or acquisition discussions, niche market focus

**Risk:** Economic downturn reduces enterprise AI spending

- **Mitigation:** Cost-effective positioning, recession-resistant use cases
- **Contingency:** Focus on free/open source adoption, extend runway

### Business Risks

**Risk:** Difficulty attracting top AI talent in competitive market

- **Mitigation:** Competitive compensation, equity, remote-first culture
- **Contingency:** University partnerships, international hiring, contractor model

**Risk:** Intellectual property disputes or patent infringement claims

- **Mitigation:** Comprehensive IP strategy, freedom to operate analysis
- **Contingency:** Defensive patent portfolio, legal insurance, settlement funds
