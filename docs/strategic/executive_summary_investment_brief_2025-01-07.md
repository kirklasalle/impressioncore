## The Opportunity

ImpressionCore is positioned to capture significant market share in the **$45.3B personal AI market** (growing to $163.6B by 2030) through its breakthrough privacy-first, edge computing approach.

- Unified Knowledge Store (UKS) mimics human memory systems

**🔒 Privacy-First Design**

- 100% local processing - no cloud dependencies
- Quantum-resistant cryptography for digital identity

**💻 Consumer Hardware Optimization**

- Targets NVIDIA GTX 1050 Ti (4GB VRAM) - 150M+ installed base
- Advanced memory optimization and 4-bit quantization

**🌐 Multimodal Integration**

- Text, image, audio, video processing in unified platform
- Seamless cross-modal understanding and generation
