The Logitech QuickCam Orbit MP (Motion Picture) was **a popular, unique webcam from the mid-2000s**, known for its spherical design, motorized head with automatic **face-tracking** (pan/tilt), **True 1.3 Megapixel sensor** for good stills, **RightLight** for low-light clarity, and built-in mic with **RightSound** (echo cancellation), connecting via USB 2.0 for video calls and recording, offering a fun, interactive experience with special effects software. \[[1](https://www.bhphotovideo.com/c/product/533298-REG/Logitech_960_000173_QuickCam_Orbit_MP_USB.html#:~:text=The%20QuickCam%20Orbit%20MP%20from%20Logitech%20is,integrated%20microphone%20allows%20you%20also%20capture%20audio.), [2](https://www.ebay.com/itm/305515564120), [3](https://ir.logitech.com/press-releases/press-release-details/2005/Logitech-Introduces-QuickCam-Orbit-MP-QuickCam-Pro-5000-Performance-Webcams/default.aspx), [4](https://hardforum.com/threads/logitech-quickcam-orbit-mp-od-33-04.1098093/latest), [5](https://www.bhphotovideo.com/c/product/561894-REG/Logitech_960_000240_QuickCam_Communicate_MP_USB.html#:~:text=The%20QuickCam%20Communicate%20MP%20from%20Logitech%20High,let%20you%20take%20up%20to%204.0MP%20stills.)\]

**Key Features:**

* **Motorized Pan/Tilt:** The camera head physically moves up/down and left/right, following you with intelligent face-tracking software.  
* **Resolution:** True 1.3 Megapixel sensor, capable of software-enhanced photos up to 4MP.  
* **Lighting Tech:** RightLight adjusts for poor lighting; RightSound reduces echo.  
* **Audio:** Integrated microphone for clear sound.  
* **Connectivity:** USB 2.0.  
* **Field of View:** Ultra-wide, covering a large area.  
* **Extras:** Included headset, adjustable stand for eye-level shots, fun video effects. \[[1](https://www.bhphotovideo.com/c/product/533298-REG/Logitech_960_000173_QuickCam_Orbit_MP_USB.html#:~:text=The%20QuickCam%20Orbit%20MP%20from%20Logitech%20is,integrated%20microphone%20allows%20you%20also%20capture%20audio.), [2](https://www.ebay.com/itm/305515564120), [3](https://ir.logitech.com/press-releases/press-release-details/2005/Logitech-Introduces-QuickCam-Orbit-MP-QuickCam-Pro-5000-Performance-Webcams/default.aspx), [4](https://hardforum.com/threads/logitech-quickcam-orbit-mp-od-33-04.1098093/latest)\]

**Why it was popular:**It made video calls more natural and engaging by tracking the user, even as they moved around, making it feel less like being "stuck" in one spot. Its distinctive look and features made it stand out. While superseded by newer tech, it was a solid performer for its time, often found at clearance prices in its era. \[[2](https://www.ebay.com/itm/305515564120), [3](https://ir.logitech.com/press-releases/press-release-details/2005/Logitech-Introduces-QuickCam-Orbit-MP-QuickCam-Pro-5000-Performance-Webcams/default.aspx), [4](https://hardforum.com/threads/logitech-quickcam-orbit-mp-od-33-04.1098093/latest), [6](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j3ty599Gz08)\]
